Open emergency help Try again

Go to a vet straight away if

  • Bleeding does not stop after 5–10 minutes of firm pressure, or blood is spurting.
  • The wound is on the chest, belly or eye, or something is stuck in it (do not pull it out).
  • Your pet is struggling to breathe, collapsed, having a seizure or may have been poisoned.

First aid for cuts and wounds

  • Keep yourself safe: a pet in pain may bite, even if it is your own.
  • If it is bleeding, press a clean cloth firmly on the wound for several minutes.
  • Rinse gently with clean water or saline. Avoid hydrogen peroxide, alcohol and human creams or medicines.
  • Stop your pet licking the wound, and keep them calm and warm.
  • Bites and punctures often hide deeper damage, so have them checked by a vet.
