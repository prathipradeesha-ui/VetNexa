## Summary

You can help an animal in need without putting yourself at risk. Frightened or injured animals may bite, and in areas with rabies, bites from unknown animals need medical attention.

## What you can do

- Keep a safe distance, and do not grab or corner a frightened animal.
- Report the animal in VetNexa Rescue with its location, condition and a photo. Registered rescuers nearby can see it.
- If it is badly injured, also contact a nearby emergency clinic.
- For a kitten alone, watch from a distance for a few hours first; the mother may return.
- If you take an animal in, keep it separate from your pets until a vet has checked it.

## Get help now if

- The animal is bleeding heavily, cannot stand, or has been hit by a vehicle.
- You are bitten or scratched: wash the wound with soap and running water for 15 minutes and see a doctor the same day.
