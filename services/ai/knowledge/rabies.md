## Summary

Rabies is a virus that is almost always fatal once signs appear, in animals and in people. It is present in Sri Lanka and many other countries and spreads through bites and scratches from infected animals. Vaccinating dogs and cats protects them and your family.

## If a person is bitten or scratched

- Wash the wound straight away with soap and plenty of running water for about 15 minutes.
- See a doctor or hospital the same day. This is a human health matter; VetNexa cannot advise on it beyond this first step.

## If your pet is bitten by another animal

- Contact a veterinarian promptly. The vet will treat the wound and check your pet's rabies vaccination status.
- Do not handle saliva from the other animal with bare hands.

## Prevention

- Keep your pet's rabies vaccination up to date, as advised by your vet and local law.
- Do not approach unknown or stray animals that behave strangely. Report them through VetNexa Rescue instead.
