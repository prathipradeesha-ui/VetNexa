## Summary

Limping can be caused by something small, like a thorn or a torn nail, or by a sprain, a fracture, an infected bite or joint disease. Cats often hide pain, so a limping cat may have an abscess from a fight.

## What you can do

- Gently check the paw and between the toes for thorns, cuts, swelling or a broken nail, if your pet allows it.
- Rest your pet: short lead walks only, and no jumping or running.
- Do not give human painkillers. They are dangerous for pets.

## See a vet if

- The limp lasts more than a day or two, or keeps coming back.
- There is swelling, heat, a wound, or your pet is in pain.

## Get help now if

- Your pet will not put any weight on the leg, the leg looks deformed, or it was hit by a vehicle or fell from a height, even if it seems fine.
