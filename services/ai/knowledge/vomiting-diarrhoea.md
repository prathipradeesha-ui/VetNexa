## Summary

A single episode of vomiting or loose stool in an otherwise bright adult pet is common and may settle on its own. Repeated vomiting, blood, or signs of being unwell need a veterinarian, because dehydration can develop quickly, especially in small, young or old pets.

## What you can do

- Make sure fresh water is always available.
- Offer small, frequent meals of a plain, easily digested food for a day or two, as your vet advises.
- Watch for the warning signs below, and do not give human anti-diarrhoea medicines.

## See a vet if

- It is a puppy, kitten or older pet, or a pet with another illness.
- Vomiting happens more than a few times or lasts more than 24 hours.
- There is blood, or your pet is tired, not eating or seems to have a painful belly.
- Your puppy is unvaccinated (there is a risk of parvovirus).

## Get help now if

- Your pet cannot keep water down, retches without bringing anything up, has a swollen belly, or may have eaten poison or an object.
