## Summary

Some everyday foods and plants can seriously poison dogs and cats, sometimes in small amounts. If your pet has eaten something dangerous, contact a veterinarian straight away, even if it seems well. Early treatment works best.

## Dangerous foods and plants

- Chocolate, coffee and other sources of caffeine.
- Grapes, raisins, sultanas and currants (they can cause kidney failure in dogs).
- Onions, garlic, leeks and chives, raw or cooked.
- Xylitol (birch sugar) in sugar-free gum, sweets and some peanut butters.
- Alcohol, macadamia nuts, and cooked bones that can splinter.
- Lilies are extremely dangerous to cats: even the pollen or the water from the vase can cause kidney failure.

## What you can do

- Call a vet immediately and say what was eaten, how much and when. Keep the packaging.
- Do not make your pet vomit unless a veterinarian tells you to.
- Keep these foods out of reach and ask visitors not to share them.
