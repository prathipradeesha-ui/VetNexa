## Summary

Eye problems can get worse very quickly and some can lead to loss of the eye. A painful eye (squinting, rubbing or keeping it closed) should be checked by a veterinarian the same day. Mild discharge with a cat flu can also need treatment.

## What you can do

- Stop your pet rubbing the eye. A cone helps.
- Gently wipe discharge away with cotton wool and cooled boiled water.
- Do not use human eye drops or ointments unless your vet prescribes them.

## See a vet if

- Your pet has a red, weepy or cloudy eye, or discharge that lasts more than a day.

## Get help now if

- Your pet is squinting or keeping an eye shut, the eye looks injured, or the eyeball bulges or seems out of place.
- Your pet suddenly seems unable to see.
