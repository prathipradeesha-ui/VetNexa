## Summary

Sneezing, a runny nose and watery eyes in cats are usually caused by "cat flu" viruses (feline herpesvirus and calicivirus). In dogs, a dry, honking cough after contact with other dogs is often kennel cough. Many cases are mild, but young, old or unvaccinated animals can become seriously ill, and both conditions are contagious.

## What you can do

- Keep your pet away from other animals until it is better.
- Gently clean discharge from the eyes and nose with cotton wool and cooled boiled water.
- Cats often stop eating when they cannot smell their food. Offer strong-smelling, slightly warmed wet food.
- Keep vaccinations up to date. They reduce how severe these infections are.

## See a vet if

- Your pet is a kitten, puppy or senior, or is not vaccinated.
- Your pet stops eating, is very tired, has thick yellow or green discharge, mouth ulcers or a fever.
- Signs last more than a few days.

## Get help now if

- Your pet is struggling to breathe, or a cat is breathing with its mouth open.
