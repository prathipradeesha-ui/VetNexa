## Summary

Rat and mouse baits, insecticides, garden chemicals, antifreeze and cleaning products are common causes of poisoning. Some act within minutes (twitching, drooling, seizures), while others, such as many rat baits, cause internal bleeding days later. Prompt treatment can be life-saving even when your pet still looks well.

## Get help now if

- You know or suspect your pet has eaten, licked or been sprayed with a poison. Call a veterinarian immediately.

## What you can do

- Take the packaging or a photo of the label to the vet.
- Do not make your pet vomit, and do not give milk, oil or salt, unless a veterinarian tells you to.
- If a chemical is on the coat, stop your pet licking it and, if the vet advises, wash it off with lukewarm water and mild washing-up liquid.
- Store chemicals securely and use pet-safe pest control.
