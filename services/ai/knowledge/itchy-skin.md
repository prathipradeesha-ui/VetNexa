## Summary

Itching and skin problems have many causes: fleas, mites (mange), allergies to food or the environment, bacterial or fungal infections (including ringworm, which can spread to people) and hormonal disease. Many look similar, so a veterinarian usually needs to examine the skin and may take simple tests.

## What you can do

- Check for fleas and keep flea prevention up to date for every pet in the home.
- Stop your pet scratching or licking damaged skin where possible.
- Do not use human creams, essential oils or home remedies on broken skin.
- Wash hands after touching patches of hair loss until ringworm is ruled out.

## See a vet if

- The skin is broken, red and moist, smelly, scabby or losing hair.
- Itching keeps your pet awake or lasts more than a few days.
- Other pets or people in the home develop itchy skin or round, scaly patches.
