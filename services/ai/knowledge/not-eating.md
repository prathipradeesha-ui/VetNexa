## Summary

A poor appetite is a common early sign of illness, pain or stress. Cats are especially at risk: a cat that eats nothing for a few days can develop a serious liver condition (hepatic lipidosis), particularly if it is overweight.

## What you can do

- Check that fresh water is available and note whether your pet is drinking.
- Offer its usual food, slightly warmed, in a quiet place. Avoid sudden diet changes.
- Look for other signs: vomiting, diarrhoea, drooling, a painful mouth, tiredness or hiding.

## See a vet if

- A cat has eaten little or nothing for 24 hours.
- A dog has not eaten for more than 24–48 hours, or sooner if it seems unwell.
- It is a puppy or kitten. Young animals can become weak quickly.
- There are any other signs of illness, or weight loss.
