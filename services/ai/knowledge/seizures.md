## Summary

A seizure can look like stiffening, paddling of the legs, chomping, drooling or loss of consciousness. Seizures can be caused by epilepsy, poisoning, low blood sugar, heatstroke, liver disease and other illnesses. A first seizure always needs a veterinary check.

## What you can do now

- Keep calm and move furniture or objects away so your pet cannot hurt itself.
- Do not put your hands or anything else in its mouth. Pets do not swallow their tongues.
- Time the seizure, keep the room quiet and dim, and record a video if you can.
- Afterwards, your pet may be confused for a while. Keep it safe and away from stairs and water.

## Get help now if

- The seizure lasts more than 5 minutes, or seizures happen one after another.
- It is your pet's first seizure, or it may have eaten poison or been overheated.
