## Summary

Intestinal worms are very common in puppies and kittens, and some can also infect people, especially children. Regular deworming with a product your vet recommends, at the right dose for your pet's weight, keeps your pet and family safer.

## Typical schedule

- Puppies and kittens: often every 2 weeks from 2–3 weeks of age until 8 weeks, then monthly until 6 months old.
- Adults: at least every 3 months, or more often if they hunt, eat raw food or live with young children.
- Your vet may adjust this to your pet's lifestyle and local parasites.

## What you can do

- Weigh your pet before dosing. Too little does not work and too much can be harmful.
- Pick up faeces promptly and wash hands after handling pets and soil.
- Treat fleas as well, because fleas spread a common tapeworm.

## See a vet if

- Your pet has diarrhoea, a swollen belly or weight loss, or is a young puppy or kitten with visible worms.
