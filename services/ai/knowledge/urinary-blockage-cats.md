## Summary

Cats, especially males, can develop a blocked urethra, so they cannot pass urine. This is a life-threatening emergency: toxins and potassium build up and can stop the heart within a day or two. Straining in the litter box can look like constipation, so it is easy to miss.

## Warning signs

- Repeated trips to the litter box producing little or no urine.
- Crying, straining or licking the genitals.
- Blood in the urine, peeing outside the litter box.
- Vomiting, hiding or weakness.

## Get help now if

- Your male cat is straining and producing little or no urine. Go to a veterinarian immediately, day or night.

## What you can do

- Encourage drinking with fresh water, water fountains and wet food, as your vet advises.
- Keep litter boxes clean, with one more box than the number of cats.
