## Summary

Core vaccines protect every dog against distemper, canine adenovirus (hepatitis) and parvovirus. Where rabies is present, including Sri Lanka, rabies vaccination is also essential and is required by law in many areas. Your veterinarian may also recommend leptospirosis vaccination, especially where dogs can contact flood water or rats.

## Typical schedule

- Puppies usually start at 6–8 weeks of age, with doses every 2–4 weeks until about 16 weeks old or older.
- A booster is given between 6 months and 1 year of age.
- Adult boosters follow your vet's plan: some vaccines are given every year (such as leptospirosis and often rabies), others less often.

## What you can do

- Keep your puppy away from unknown dogs and public dog areas until the vet says the course is complete.
- Record every vaccination in VetNexa health records so you are reminded when boosters are due.

## See a vet if

- Your dog has facial swelling, vomiting, collapse or breathing difficulty after a vaccine (rare, but urgent).
- You are not sure whether an adopted dog has been vaccinated. The vet can restart a course safely.
