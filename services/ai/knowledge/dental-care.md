## Summary

Most adult dogs and cats develop some gum disease. It causes pain and bad breath and can affect general health. Daily tooth brushing is the most effective care at home, and regular dental checks catch problems early.

## What you can do

- Brush daily with a soft pet toothbrush or finger brush and toothpaste made for pets. Human toothpaste can contain xylitol or fluoride, which are harmful when swallowed.
- Start slowly with short, positive sessions and rewards.
- Ask your vet about dental diets or chews with proven benefit.
- Have your pet's mouth checked at least once a year.

## See a vet if

- You notice bad breath, red or bleeding gums, broken or loose teeth, or brown tartar.
- Your pet drops food, chews on one side, paws at its mouth or drools.
