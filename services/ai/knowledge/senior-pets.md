## Summary

Dogs and cats are usually considered senior from around 7–10 years of age, depending on size and breed. Many changes blamed on "old age", such as stiffness, weight loss or drinking more, are signs of treatable conditions like arthritis, kidney disease, diabetes or an overactive thyroid.

## What you can do

- Have a health check every 6 months, including blood and urine tests if your vet advises.
- Provide soft bedding, non-slip floors, ramps, and easy access to food, water and litter trays.
- Keep your pet at a healthy weight and give gentle, regular exercise.
- Check for new lumps monthly and note their size and place.

## See a vet if

- Your pet drinks or urinates more, loses weight, is stiff or limping, or seems confused.
- You find a new lump, or an existing one changes.
