## Summary

Venomous snakes such as cobras, vipers and kraits are found in Sri Lanka and other tropical countries. Snake bites are emergencies for dogs and cats. Signs may appear quickly or over several hours, and antivenom and supportive care work best when given early.

## Get help now if

- You saw or suspect a snake bite. Go to a veterinarian immediately, even if your pet seems fine at first.

## What you can do now

- Keep your pet as calm and still as possible, and carry it if you can.
- Keep yourself safe: do not try to catch or kill the snake. A photo from a safe distance can help.
- Do not cut the wound, suck out venom, apply ice or tie a tight tourniquet.
- Remove a tight collar if the head or neck is swelling.
