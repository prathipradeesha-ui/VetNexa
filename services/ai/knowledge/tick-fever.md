## Summary

Tick fever is a group of blood infections carried by ticks and is common in dogs in tropical countries. It can start with mild tiredness and fever and become life-threatening if it damages blood cells and platelets. It needs a blood test and treatment from a veterinarian.

## Common signs

- Tiredness, fever and poor appetite.
- Pale gums, weakness or breathlessness.
- Bleeding: nosebleeds, bruises, or blood in urine or faeces.
- Dark or red-brown urine.

## What you can do

- Do not wait for it to pass. Arrange a vet visit soon.
- Keep your dog rested and cool until it is seen.
- Use regular tick prevention for every dog in the home.

## Get help now if

- Your dog collapses, has very pale or white gums, is bleeding or is breathing hard.
