## Summary

The first weeks at home shape your pet's health and behaviour for life. A vet check soon after arrival, vaccinations, deworming and gentle socialisation give a puppy or kitten the best start.

## First steps

- Book a vet check within the first week, and bring any vaccination and deworming records.
- Add your pet to VetNexa and record its treatments so you get reminders.
- Ask about vaccination, deworming, flea control, microchipping and neutering.

## What you can do

- Introduce new people, sounds and handling calmly and positively during the first months.
- Reward toilet training outside, or provide a clean litter tray in a quiet place.
- Feed a complete puppy or kitten food in several small meals a day.

## See a vet if

- Your puppy or kitten is tired, not eating, vomiting, has diarrhoea or is not gaining weight. Young animals can become ill very quickly.
