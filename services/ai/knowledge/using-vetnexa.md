## Summary

VetNexa brings your pet's care together in one place. You can find clinics, book consultations with verified veterinarians, keep health records with reminders, get a cautious first look at a wound, and help animals through rescue and adoption.

## Where to find things

- **Find a clinic:** the Find a clinic page lists clinics by city and services. Use the emergency filter for 24-hour care.
- **Book a consultation:** in your dashboard, open Consultations and choose a vet, pet and time.
- **Health records and reminders:** open your pet in My pets and add vaccinations, treatments and check-ups. Due dates appear as reminders.
- **Wound check:** upload a photo of a skin wound on your dog or cat for a cautious, AI-assisted first look.
- **Rescue and adoption:** report an animal in need, or browse animals waiting for a home.

## About this assistant

- This assistant gives general pet-care information from a written knowledge base. It cannot examine your pet or diagnose illness.
- For anything urgent, contact a veterinarian straight away.
