## Summary

Canine parvovirus is a highly contagious and often fatal virus that mostly affects puppies and unvaccinated dogs. It attacks the gut and the immune system. With early, intensive veterinary treatment many puppies survive; without treatment many do not.

## Common signs

- Sudden tiredness and loss of appetite.
- Repeated vomiting.
- Severe, often bloody and foul-smelling diarrhoea.

## What you can do

- Contact a veterinarian immediately and say you suspect parvo, so the clinic can protect other patients.
- Keep the dog away from other dogs. The virus survives for months in the environment.
- Clean hard surfaces with a disinfectant that kills parvovirus. Your vet can advise; diluted household bleach is often used.

## Prevention

- Complete the full puppy vaccination course and keep boosters up to date.
