## Summary

Difficulty breathing is always serious. Cats rarely pant, so a cat breathing with its mouth open or with big belly movements needs help immediately. Sneezing or a mild cough in an otherwise bright pet is usually less urgent; see the article on cat flu and kennel cough.

## Get help now if

- Your pet is struggling to breathe, breathing fast at rest, gasping, or has pale, grey or blue gums or tongue.
- A cat is breathing with its mouth open.
- Your pet is choking and cannot breathe.

## What you can do

- Keep your pet calm, cool and still, and travel to the vet with good air flow.
- If your pet is choking, look in its mouth only if it is safe and you can clearly see the object. Do not push your fingers blindly down the throat.

## See a vet if

- A cough lasts more than a few days, or your pet is tired, not eating or has a fever.
