## Summary

Adopting gives a homeless animal a second chance. Before you apply, think about the time, space, cost and care an animal will need for its whole life, which can be 12–20 years.

## How adoption works on VetNexa

- Browse animals on the Adopt page and filter by species, size and location.
- Open a listing and send an application describing your home and experience.
- The rescuer reviews applications and contacts suitable adopters. You can follow your applications in your dashboard.

## Before your pet arrives

- Prepare a quiet space, bed, food and water bowls, a litter tray for cats, and a secure lead and collar for dogs.
- Pet-proof your home: hide cables, medicines and toxic plants.
- Book a vet check within the first week and add your new pet to VetNexa.
