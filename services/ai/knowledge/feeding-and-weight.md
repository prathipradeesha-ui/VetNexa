## Summary

Pets need a complete and balanced diet for their life stage (growing, adult or senior). Cats are strict carnivores and need nutrients such as taurine that dog food and most home-cooked diets do not provide. Being overweight shortens a pet's life and makes arthritis, diabetes and heatstroke more likely.

## What you can do

- Choose a complete food for your pet's species and life stage, and use the feeding guide as a starting point.
- Weigh food rather than guessing, and keep treats to less than about 10% of daily calories.
- Always provide fresh water.
- Check body shape: you should be able to feel the ribs easily under a thin layer of fat and see a waist from above.
- Ask your vet before starting a home-made diet, so it can be properly balanced.

## See a vet if

- Your pet loses or gains weight without a change in diet.
- Your pet starts drinking or urinating much more than usual.
