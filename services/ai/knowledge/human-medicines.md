## Summary

Many human medicines are dangerous for pets. Paracetamol can kill a cat even in small amounts, and ibuprofen, naproxen, diclofenac and aspirin can cause stomach ulcers and kidney failure in dogs and cats. Pets process drugs differently from people, so only a veterinarian can choose a safe medicine and dose.

## What you can do

- Do not give any human medicine, cream or eye drop unless your veterinarian has prescribed it for this pet.
- If your pet is in pain, contact a vet. There are pain medicines made for pets.
- Store medicines in closed cupboards, because pets often chew blister packs.

## Get help now if

- Your pet has swallowed any human medicine. Call a vet immediately with the name, strength and number of tablets.
